# Composite score removed

[← Home](Home.md)

Structure Break Signals v8.0 has no confidence score, score cutoff, score
tuning, or score-based alert filter. The former score combined correlated chart
measurements into an unsupported 0–100 ranking.

Use the directly exported facts instead: break clearance, candle range, body
ratio, volume multiple, source-pivot age, ATR context, and session context.
None of those fields predicts or recommends a trade.
